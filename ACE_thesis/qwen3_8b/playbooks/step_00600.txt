## Numerical Strategies

- [ns-00001] When calculating percentage change, use subtract(new_value, old_value), divide(#0, old_value) to ensure correct sign.
- [ns-00002] When calculating interest expense, divide(rate, const_100), multiply(#0, notional_amount) to ensure correct magnitude.
## Table Reading Rules

- [tr-00001] When calculating net change involving negative entries, use add(positive_entry, negative_entry), add(#0, another_negative) to maintain correct sign.
## Program Generation

- [pg-00001] When comparing performance or values, use greater(value1, value2) instead of subtract(value1, value2).
- [pg-00002] When calculating total amounts over multiple years, use add(year1, year2), add(#0, year3), add(#1, year4) without extra operations.
